## About The Project

StudentBuddy is a digital platform designed primarily for university students, but also for professors, teaching assistants, and academic staff, 
aiming to foster collaboration, knowledge sharing, and mutual support throughout the academic journey.

## Overview

The application allows different users to:
- **Students** can pload and access notes, study materials, and exam tips; share advice on courses and exams; participate in peer discussions.
- **Professors** and **Teaching Assistants** can Share official course materials, provide announcements, and engage with students in forums.
- **PhD Students** and **Researcher** can upload surveys, collect and analyze results to support their thesis and research projects.
- **App Moderator** can moderate the posts, approve materials etc..

The platform is organized by university, faculty, degree program, and individual courses to streamline navigation and resource discovery.

## Features

- Resource Sharing: Upload and browse notes, lecture slides, official documents, and study materials.
- Peer and Faculty Support: Forums for thematic discussions involving students, professors, and assistants.
- Academic Events: Announcements and notifications for university-related events and deadlines.
- Self-assessment Tools: Features to help students evaluate their preparation and progress (Such like flashcards etc..)
- Exam & Schedule Planning: Tools to organize exam dates, course timetables, and academic calendars.

## Goals

StudentBuddy aims to create a supportive academic community where students and faculty can collaborate, exchange resources and advice, and improve the overall educational experience.
